# Source review

Source: [Righting Software](<../references/Righting Software.pdf>), file identity in [source authority](../governance/source-authority.md).

## Completed review scope

All fourteen chapters and Appendices A–C were reviewed, including the worked system- and project-design examples. Preface and back matter were inspected for scope and navigation. Text extraction supported reading; extraction alone was not treated as review. Forty-eight selected rendered pages were visually inspected, including lifecycle diagrams, networks, graphs, and numerical expressions. This does not claim pixel-by-pixel inspection of every figure.

## Numerical verification scope

The official companion archive and publisher errata were inspected as recorded in [source authority](../governance/source-authority.md). Selected workbook formulas and cached values were read without executing macros. Independent arithmetic checks covered illustrative criticality, arithmetic activity risk, geometric criticality, crossover scaling, risk-curve inflection, crossover residuals, and duration-weighted progress. This is not validation of every companion workbook or a production calculator. Graphical errata were not all independently rendered.

The printed scaling-factor discrepancy was escalated. The owner authorized the companion calculation and preservation of the discrepancy. The owner also selected token-based resource costing; worker calendar and availability remain unresolved.

## Supplementary sources

The IDesign management overview supports the distinction between system design, project design, and detailed design. The supplied InformIT decomposition and risk excerpts overlap the reviewed book. The New Standard for Software Design article supports retaining the book's rationale when applying a checklist. The O’Reilly architecture article body was unavailable in the retrieved page; no new rule was imported from it. The CODE interface article was inspected in part, not reviewed in full. The remaining historical technology articles are cataloged in [references](../references/README.md); they have not established a technology baseline or been claimed as fully reviewed.

A newer online design-standard download was not obtained or adopted. No registration information was submitted. Appendix C of the repository edition remains the baseline.
